My Projects ▼

Nonlinear Solvers

  • This is my bachelors thesis on numerical methods for solving nonlinear equations and systems of nonlinear equations, which are common in science, engineering, and optimization.
  • It covers both single-variable methods, such as Bisection, Secant, Newton, Damped Newton, Adaptive Damped Newton, and Brent’s method, and multivariable methods, including Newton’s method for systems and Broyden’s method.
  • The project explains the theory behind each method, including convergence, stability, and when they may fail or become unreliable.
  • All methods are implemented in Python in a modular form and return structured results such as convergence status, residuals, and iteration history.
  • Numerical experiments compare the methods in terms of accuracy, speed, and robustness using practical test cases and residual analysis.
  • The thesis also connects nonlinear solvers to optimization ideas, especially line search, damping, and Armijo-type conditions.
